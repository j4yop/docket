# Third-Party Notices

are original work built for the Clocked Build Challenge 2026.

The marketing page at `/` is a static mirrored layout used as a demo surface.
All third-party brand names, logos, customer marks, testimonials, blog posts
and social links from the reference shell have been removed or replaced. The
remaining shell primitives are generic utility CSS, geometric hover overlays
and a noise texture; they carry no third-party brand marks.
